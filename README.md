## Featured Projects

- **⭐ [Universal-AlderLake-IrisXe](https://github.com/Aniket601/Universal-AlderLake-IrisXe)**: The Universal OpenCore EFI generator, preconfigured engine, and guide for Intel 12th Gen (Alder Lake) & 13th Gen (Raptor Lake) laptops with full Metal 2 Iris Xe hardware acceleration across Dell, HP, Lenovo, and Asus. Features an interactive CLI configurator and automated CI validation.
- **[AlderLake-IrisXe-OpenCore](https://github.com/Aniket601/AlderLake-IrisXe-OpenCore)**: Unified OpenCore EFI for Lenovo IdeaPad 3 15IAU7 running **macOS Tahoe 26.7.1** and **macOS Ventura 13.7.8** with full Metal 2 Iris Xe graphics acceleration, Wi-Fi 6, ALC257 audio, and battery management.
